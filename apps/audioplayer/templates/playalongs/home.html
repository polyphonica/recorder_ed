Recorder-ed Editions

Our editions of early music are prepared from original prints and manuscripts, with editorial suggestions clearly marked. They're free to download with an account, for personal study and teaching.

Some play-alongs are of music still in copyright. For those we provide the recording, with the publisher's permission, and tell you where to buy the music.
