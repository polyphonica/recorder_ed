About our editions & copyright

Recorder-ed Editions

Music by composers who died more than 70 years ago is out of copyright. For these pieces we prepare our own Recorder-ed Editions, working from original prints, manuscripts or facsimiles rather than from modern editions.

Each edition names its source. Anything we have added, such as ornaments, breathing marks or suggested slurs, is shown as an editorial suggestion, so you can tell it apart from what the composer wrote.

How you may use them

  • Download and print them for your own study and practice.
  • Teachers may use them with their own pupils.
  • Please don't share the files publicly, upload them elsewhere, or sell them. Link to the play-along page instead.

Downloading an edition needs a free Recorder-ed account.

Music still in copyright

Some play-alongs are of music that is still in copyright. We only publish these recordings with the permission of the copyright holder, and we never include the sheet music.

To play along you'll need your own copy. Each of these pieces tells you which publication it comes from and where to buy it. Buying the music supports the composers and publishers who make it available.

Questions

If you hold the rights to a piece and have a question about how it is used here, please contact us.
